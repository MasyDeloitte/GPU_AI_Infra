Method / on-prem edition

Excel-derived sizing chain

InfraPilot AI follows the workbook relationship from workload demand through model selection, on-prem GPU/server capacity, BOM, and financial planning. Every scenario can be tuned before a management decision is recorded.

01

Demand

Derives query, output, history, retrieval, tool, context, model-call, and token-per-second requirements.

02

Capacity

Combines VRAM, throughput, prefill, decode, concurrency, and tensor-parallel minimums using the workbook's maximum-driver rule.

03

Decision

Compares minimum, moderate, and heavy options. Users can change the model, GPU quantity, cost assumptions, growth, HA, and DR before saving a scenario.
